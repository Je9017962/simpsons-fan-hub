Character Guide

Meet the residents of Springfield! From the Simpson family to their unforgettable neighbors, get to know the characters who make this show a classic.

Homer Simpson

Homer is the lovable, donut-obsessed dad of the Simpson family. He works at the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant and is known for his hilarious mistakes and his famous catchphrase, "D'oh!"

Marge Simpson

Marge is the heart of the Simpson family. Known for her iconic blue hair and endless patience, she keeps everyone together while navigating the chaos of life in Springfield.

Bart Simpson

Bart is the mischievous 10-year-old troublemaker who loves skateboarding, pulling pranks, and keeping everyone on their toes. His famous catchphrase is "Eat my shorts!"

Lisa Simpson

Lisa is the intelligent and passionate middle child of the family. She loves playing her saxophone, standing up for what she believes in, and making the world a better place.

Maggie Simpson

Maggie may be the youngest Simpson, but she has plenty of personality! She's usually seen with her pacifier and often surprises everyone with her clever actions.
